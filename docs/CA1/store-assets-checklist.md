# Store Asset Checklist

| Asset            | Requirement                                                                                                            | Plan                                                                                                         |
| :--------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| App icon         | 512 x 512 px, 32-bit PNG with alpha, maximum 1024 KB                                                                   | Create an icon that represents Forward and matches the visual style of the game.                             |
| Feature graphic  | 1024 x 500 px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha                                                                        | Create a promotional graphic showing the visual style and theme of Forward.                                  |
| Phone screenshot | JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha, 320-3840 px. The maximum dimension must not be more than twice the minimum dimesion. | Capture at least three portrait gameplay screenshots at 1080 x 1920 or higher when the gameplay is complete. |
## Screenshot plan
1. The player running through a dream-like city environment.
2.  The player moving through a different dream environment, such as a corridor or rooftop.
3.  A later or more difficult section of the dream showing a different environment.

The screenshots will show the actual in-game experience and will not include misleading promotional content.
